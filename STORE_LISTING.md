## Detailed Description

### Drowning in tabs? Let AI organize them for you.

AI Tab Manager uses Google Gemini AI to intelligently group your browser tabs by topic, summarize web pages, and save research sessions for later.

**Key Features:**

- **AI Auto-Group** — One click to automatically organize all your tabs into color-coded groups by topic
- **Group by Domain** — Instantly group tabs by website (no AI needed, works offline)
- **Tab Summaries** — Get a quick 2-3 sentence summary of any webpage
- **AI Search** — Search across all your open tabs by meaning, not just keywords
- **Save Sessions** — Save your current tabs as a session and restore them anytime
- **Side Panel** — Full tab management UI in Chrome's side panel

**Why AI Tab Manager?**

- Works with your own Gemini API key (free tier available from Google)
- Privacy-first: no data is sent to our servers, ever
- Lightweight: under 100KB total size
- Dark mode support
- Open source

**Perfect for:**
- Researchers managing dozens of reference tabs
- Developers juggling multiple project contexts  
- Students organizing study materials
- Anyone with too many tabs open

**Getting Started:**
1. Install the extension
2. Get a free Gemini API key from aistudio.google.com
3. Open Settings and paste your key
4. Click "AI Auto-Group" and watch the magic!

**Permissions Explained:**
- "Read your browsing history" — needed to read tab titles and URLs for grouping
- "View and manage tab groups" — needed to create and organize tab groups
- "Read and change all your data on all websites" — needed to extract page content for AI summarization (only when you explicitly request it)
